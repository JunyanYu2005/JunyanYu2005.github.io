Teaching and Mentoring

  • October 2026–present

    Part-time Teaching Assistant

    Putonghua Education Research and Development Centre, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

    In the Putonghua Language and Vocabulary Programme, I facilitate vocabulary and oral communication classes for Primary 4–6 pupils. Working alongside lead instructors, I assist with classroom management, lead interactive speaking drills, and provide individualised feedback tailored to Cantonese-speaking learners to enhance their phonological accuracy, lexical usage, and expressive fluency.

  • June–August 2026

    Part-time General Teacher

    Evergreen Way Education Centre (Ocean Pride), Hong Kong

    I delivered structured tutoring for primary and secondary students across Chinese Language, Chinese Literature, Putonghua, English Language, and Mathematics, designing lesson materials aligned with local Hong Kong school curricula. In Chinese lessons, I diagnosed students’ grammatical and lexical challenges through contrastive Cantonese–Mandarin analysis, providing targeted coaching to improve reading comprehension, textual interpretation, and writing proficiency.

  • September 2024–present

    Part-time Mentor

    Department of Chinese Language and Literature, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

    Through the Collaborative Approach for Language Learning (CALL) programme, I conduct individual writing consultations for Year 1–3 undergraduates. I guide students in refining thesis statements, structuring coherent arguments, selecting relevant textual evidence, and articulating close analytical readings in academic essays.
