Teaching and mentoring

  • October 2026–present

    Putonghua Teaching Assistant

    Putonghua Education Research and Development Centre, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

    I help Primary 4–6 pupils practise speaking in the Putonghua Language and Vocabulary Programme. I give Cantonese-speaking pupils individual feedback on pronunciation and on how to use new vocabulary in conversation.

  • June–August 2026

    Part-time General Teacher

    Evergreen Way Education Centre (Ocean Pride), Hong Kong

    I tutored primary and secondary students in Chinese language, Chinese literature, Putonghua, English and mathematics. In Chinese lessons I used contrasts between Cantonese and Mandarin to help students interpret passages and revise their writing.

  • September 2024–present

    Academic Writing Mentor

    Department of Chinese Language and Literature, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

    Through the Collaborative Approach for Language Learning programme, I work with undergraduates in Years 1–3 on organising an argument, choosing evidence and explaining how it supports a reading.
