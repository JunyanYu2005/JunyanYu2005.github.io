Interests

I study aspect in Chinese dialects. My work on Shaoxing Wu asks how sentence type and discourse context affect the choice of progressive and durative markers. In Cantonese, I am interested in how the verbal suffix -lok (落) combines with verbs to mark the onset of an event or state.

My broader interests include the syntax, semantics and pragmatics of tense and aspect, classical Chinese literature, philology and comparative literary theory.
