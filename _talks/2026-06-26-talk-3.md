This talk presents findings on the progressive aspect markers *leda*, *ledoŋ*, and *lehaŋ* in modern Shaoxing dialect, examining their distribution, semantic distinctions, and pragmatic functions. Presented at the 9th International Forum on Linguistics and Chinese Education, Hong Kong.
