This paper investigates the syntactic, semantic, and pragmatic properties of the progressive aspect markers *leda*, *ledoŋ*, and *lehaŋ* in modern Shaoxing dialect, contributing to our understanding of aspect marking in Wu Chinese varieties. Presented at the 12th Inter-Regional Forum on Dialectal Grammar, Shanghai.
