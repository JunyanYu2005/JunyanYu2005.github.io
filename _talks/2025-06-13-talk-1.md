This paper explores Lu Xun's short story *Taming the Floods* in dialogue with Brecht's theory of Epic Theatre, examining how the narrative strategies of Lu Xun's fiction anticipate and resonate with Brechtian dramatic techniques. Presented at the 9th Undergraduate Academic Conference on Humanities, Hong Kong.
